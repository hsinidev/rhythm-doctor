### Game Details & Guide
Rhythm Doctor Guide



Game Overview


The Rhythm Doctor is a browser-based game that challenges players to match rhythm notes on a virtual piano.


In this game, you will control the white keys, playing in time with the music to earn points and progress through levels.


The goal is to match three adjacent notes in a row (or column) before the music ends. The game requires quick reflexes and timing skills to succeed.



How to Play


To play the Rhythm Doctor, simply click on any key or group of keys on the piano display.


The game will automatically generate a rhythm note for you to match. You can also use the "Hint" button to get a visual cue about what notes to press next.


As you progress through levels, the difficulty increases, and the rhythms become more complex. You'll need to improve your timing and accuracy to beat the clock.

Tips and Tricks for Success


Here are some tips to help you improve your gameplay:



  

Practice makes perfect
: The more you play, the better you'll get at matching rhythms.

  

Use the Hint button sparingly
